A payment is a charge against a payer for a fixed amount in BRL. YuvexPay
exposes a single `POST /v1/payments` endpoint that creates payments across
three methods (`PIX`, `CARD`, `BOLETO`) and two presentation modes (`headless`
and `hosted`).

## Methods at a glance

| Method | Headless | Hosted |
|--------|----------|--------|
| `PIX`     | Returns the BR-Code (copy-paste) and a QR code image. You render them in your own UI. | Returns a `checkoutUrl` to the YuvexPay-hosted checkout. |
| `CARD`    | Not available. Use the hosted checkout. | The payer enters card details on YuvexPay's hosted page. See [Card payments](#card-payments). |
| `BOLETO`  | Returns a boleto barcode and a downloadable PDF. | Available through the hosted checkout. |

Pick `headless` when you want to render the payment yourself; pick `hosted`
when you want YuvexPay to render the checkout, including method selection.

## Amounts and currency

- All amounts are in **BRL** as decimal numbers (e.g. `49.90`), not cents.
- The minimum chargeable amount is `R$ 0.01`. The maximum is `R$ 1,000,000.00`.
- Card payments go through the hosted checkout only. They are charged to a credit card in a single installment (à vista) or to a debit card, and need an `amount` of at least `R$ 5.00`.
- With [line items](#line-items), the amount is the sum of the lines. A [coupon](#coupons) lowers it, and on the hosted checkout the payer can change it before picking a method by editing quantities, ticking the [order bump](#order-bump) or applying a coupon. Read the final `amount` from `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` or the webhook.

## Identifiers

Every payment has two identifiers:

- `id` — an internal UUID. Use it as the path parameter for `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` and `POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund`.
- `txId` — a stable, prefixed transaction id (`PAY` + 32 hex characters) that identifies the payment across logs, reports and the dashboard.

You can also pass `externalId` on creation — typically your own order id —
which is stored on the payment for cross-referencing. `externalId` is unique
per company and environment.

## Lifecycle

```
NEW
  └── PROCESSING ──► CONFIRMED ──► PAID ──► PARTIAL_REFUND ──► REFUNDED
                                       │
                                       ├── CHARGEBACK
                                       └── MED_FROZEN
  └── PENDING_METHOD_SELECTION ──► (one of the above, after the payer picks a method)
                               ├──► FREE (a coupon covered the whole amount and the payer confirmed)
                               └──► EXPIRED (no method picked before expiresAt)
  └── FREE (headless payment whose coupon covered the whole amount)
  └── EXPIRED
  └── CANCELLED
```

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `NEW` | Created and waiting for the payer. |
| `PENDING_METHOD_SELECTION` | A hosted multi-method checkout is created and the payer hasn't picked a method yet. |
| `PROCESSING` | The card or PIX network is processing the charge, or the outcome of a boleto charge is not known yet after a `502` (see [Boleto errors](#boleto-errors)). |
| `CONFIRMED` | The payment was authorized but funds are not yet settled (typical for cards/boleto). |
| `PAID` | Funds were received and credited to your balance. |
| `EXPIRED` | The payment window passed without payment. |
| `CANCELLED` | The payment was cancelled before being paid. |
| `PARTIAL_REFUND` | A partial refund has been issued. No further refund is possible. |
| `REFUNDED` | A full refund has been issued. |
| `CHARGEBACK` | A chargeback was filed and processed. If the card dispute is won, the payment returns to the status it had before the chargeback. |
| `MED_FROZEN` | A PIX MED (special refund) is open; funds are temporarily held. |
| `FREE` | A coupon discounted the whole amount and the order completed without a charge. It is final and cannot be refunded. See [Free orders](#free-orders). |

Listen for the corresponding [webhook events](/guides/webhooks) to react to
status changes asynchronously.

## Headless PIX

Headless PIX is the simplest flow. Create the payment, render the QR code or
copy-paste string, and listen for `PAYMENT_PAID`:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.yuvexpay.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $YUVEX_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-Idempotency-Key: order-1234" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 49.90,
    "methods": ["PIX"],
    "currency": "BRL",
    "mode": "headless",
    "description": "Order #1234",
    "externalId": "order-1234",
    "expiresInMinutes": 30,
    "customer": {
      "name": "Pedro Álvares Cabral",
      "email": "user@example.com"
    }
  }'
```

The response includes `methodData.pixCopyPaste` (the BR-Code string) and
`methodData.qrCodeBase64` (a data URI). Render either to the payer.

A PIX payment whose create returns `502` or `503 PROVIDER_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`
is recorded as `CANCELLED`: the charge was not created, so retry with a new
`externalId`.

## Boleto errors

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| `400 PROVIDER_REJECTED` | The payment was refused. The payment is recorded as `CANCELLED`. The message is generic and does not say why. |
| `503 PROVIDER_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE` | The charge was not created. The payment is recorded as `CANCELLED`. Retry later. |
| `502 PROVIDER_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE` | The outcome is not known yet. The payment stays `PROCESSING` until it is resolved, which can take 15 minutes or more, and then either continues normally or ends `CANCELLED`. |

`methodData` is `null` while a boleto payment is `PROCESSING` after a `502`,
and on a payment recorded `CANCELLED` before a charge was created.

A create request that reuses an `externalId` returns the existing payment
instead of creating another one. Send `externalId` on every boleto payment.
After a `400` or a `503`, use a new `externalId` to try again.

The `502` is stored for its `X-Idempotency-Key`: repeating the request with the
same `X-Idempotency-Key` returns the same `502` for 24 hours and never submits
a second charge. To recover the payment after a `502`:

1. Repeat the request once with the same `externalId` and a new
   `X-Idempotency-Key`. The response is the existing payment with its `id`, and
   no second charge is submitted. Its `methodData` is `null` while it is
   `PROCESSING`.
2. Poll `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` until the status leaves `PROCESSING`. Do
   not poll by repeating the create: a repeated create with the same key
   replays the same response for 24 hours.
3. If the payment ends `CANCELLED`, create a new payment with a new
   `externalId`.

Without `externalId`, a request with a new `X-Idempotency-Key` creates a new
payment, and the payer can end up with two boletos for the same order.

## Line items

Send `items` on `POST /v1/payments` to charge several lines in one payment. The
payment `amount` is the sum of `unitAmount × quantity`, so you can leave
`amount` out:

```json
{
  "items": [
    { "name": "Camiseta", "unitAmount": 59.90, "quantity": 2, "sku": "TSHIRT-M" },
    { "name": "Boné", "unitAmount": 39.90, "quantity": 1, "productId": "8a3f1c2e-6b4d-4e7a-9c1b-2d3e4f5a6b7c" }
  ],
  "methods": ["PIX", "CARD"],
  "mode": "hosted",
  "itemsQuantityEditable": true
}
```

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| `name` | 1 to 120 characters, shown to the payer. |
| `unitAmount` | Unit price in BRL, greater than `0`, with at most 2 decimal places. |
| `quantity` | An integer from `1` to `999`. |
| `productId` | Optional. A product of your account in the environment of the key; any other id returns `400 ITEM_PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`. [Coupons](#coupons) limited to products match lines by this id. |
| `sku` | Optional. Your own code for the line, 1 to 64 characters. |

- `items` takes 1 to 50 lines.
- When you also send `amount`, it must equal the sum of the lines exactly.
  Otherwise the request fails with `400 AMOUNT_ITEMS_MISMATCH`, and
  `details.itemsTotal` carries the sum as a string, such as `"159.70"`.
- The sum must be at most `R$ 1,000,000.00` (`400 AMOUNT_TOO_HIGH`), and at
  least `R$ 5.00` when `methods` has `CARD` or `BOLETO`
  (`400 MINIMUM_AMOUNT_FOR_CARD_OR_BOLETO`).
- `itemsQuantityEditable: true` lets the payer change the quantity of each line
  on the hosted checkout before picking a method. `amount`, the coupon
  discount, the fee and the percentage splits follow the change. It has no
  effect without `items` or on a payment with a `FIXED` split.

Payment responses carry the lines and the totals:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `items` | The lines, each with `id`, `name`, `unitAmount`, `quantity`, `kind`, `productId` and `sku`. `[]` on a payment created with an amount only, until the payer ticks an order bump. |
| `subtotal` | The amount before the coupon. |
| `discount` | The coupon discount, `0` without one. |
| `coupon` | `{ "code": "BLACK10" }`, or `null`. |
| `amount` | `subtotal` minus `discount`, before any fee passed to the payer. |

`kind` is `ITEM` for a line you sent, `ORDER_BUMP` for the
[order bump](#order-bump) the payer ticked, and `UPSELL` for the line of a
payment created by a [post-purchase upsell](#post-purchase-upsell). Webhook
bodies do not carry the lines: read them with
`GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}`.

## Coupons

Create coupons in the dashboard, under **Vendas &gt; Cupons**, or with the
[Coupons API](/api-reference/coupons/create). A payment takes a coupon in two
ways:

- `couponCode` on `POST /v1/payments`.
- The **Cupom** (Coupon) field of the hosted checkout, shown while the payer
  has not picked a method and no coupon is applied, and only when your account
  has at least one active coupon that can apply to that payment: inside its
  validity window, with uses left, covering the whole order or a product of the
  payment, and of the payment's environment. The page learns only whether a
  coupon can apply, never which codes exist. The per-payer limit does not hide
  the field: it is checked as described below.

A coupon is `PERCENT`, with `percentBp` in basis points from `1` to `10000`
(`1000` is 10%), or `FIXED`, with an `amount` in BRL. The discount is computed
in centavos on the lines the coupon covers: a percentage rounds half up, and a
fixed amount never goes beyond the total of those lines. `productIds` limits
the coupon to the lines with those products, the order bump included when its
product is in the list; empty, the coupon covers the whole order. On a payment
without `items`, the coupon covers the `amount` when `productIds` is empty or
lists the payment's `productId`.

With the order above and a 10% coupon `BLACK10`, `subtotal` is `159.70`,
`discount` is `15.97` and `amount` is `143.73`.

- **One answer for every refusal.** `400 COUPON_INVALID` is returned for a code
  that does not exist, is inactive or deleted, is outside its validity window,
  has no uses left, is over the per-payer limit, or gives no discount on this
  order. Codes are case-insensitive.
- **Validity.** `validFrom` is inclusive and `validUntil` exclusive.
- **Uses.** `maxRedemptions` counts the payments that hold the coupon plus the
  completed ones, and the coupon's `redemptionCount` shows that number.
  Applying the coupon takes one use. The use is given back when the payment
  expires or is cancelled, and kept when it is paid or completes as `FREE`, so
  an unpaid checkout holds its use until `expiresAt`. A payment paid after it
  expired takes its use back, even past the limit.
- **Per payer.** `maxRedemptionsPerBuyer` counts the uses per payer e-mail, and
  is checked only when the e-mail is known: on `POST /v1/payments` with
  `customer.email`, and on the hosted checkout when the payer picks a method.
  If the payer is over the limit at that point, the checkout removes the coupon
  and shows the full price. A headless payment created without
  `customer.email` is not checked.
- **Environments.** A coupon belongs to the environment it was created in: a
  `ypk_test_` key manages sandbox coupons and a `ypk_live_` key production
  ones, and a payment uses only coupons of its own environment.
- **Before a method is picked.** On the hosted checkout, the payer applies or
  removes a coupon only before picking a method. A payment that already holds a
  coupon keeps it when you edit, deactivate or delete the coupon.
- **Splits.** A payment with a `FIXED` split never takes a coupon. The discount
  lowers `amount`, and the percentage splits with it. On the hosted checkout, a
  coupon whose discount would take the splits over `netAmount` is refused with
  `COUPON_INVALID`.
- **Methods.** When the discount takes the total below `R$ 5.00`, the hosted
  checkout offers PIX only, and a headless boleto fails with
  `400 MINIMUM_AMOUNT_FOR_CARD_OR_BOLETO`.

A coupon that covers the whole amount turns the payment into a
[free order](#free-orders).

## Free orders

When a coupon covers the whole amount, the payment completes with no charge
and ends `FREE`:

- A headless payment completes on `POST /v1/payments` itself. The `201`
  response has `status: "FREE"`, `amount: 0` and `methodData: null`.
- A hosted payment stays `PENDING_METHOD_SELECTION`, with `amount: 0`, until
  the payer confirms the order on the checkout, which asks for no payment
  method. A payment that is never confirmed expires like any other, and its
  coupon use is given back.

On a `FREE` payment:

- `amount`, `feeAmount` and `netAmount` are `0`, `discount` equals `subtotal`,
  `feePassedToPayer` is `false`, `paidAt` is `null` and `completedAt` is the
  time the order completed.
- Nothing was charged, so nothing can be refunded:
  `POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund` returns `400 INVALID_STATUS`, and the
  payer cannot request a refund on the purchase page.
- YuvexPay charges a fee for each free order: the fixed part of your PIX fee,
  without the percentage and without a minimum, and nothing when your PIX fee
  has no fixed part. It is never passed to the payer. It is debited from your
  balance when the order completes, even if the balance goes negative, and
  appears in [`GET /v1/documents/statements`](/api-reference/documents/statement)
  as **Taxa de pedido gratuito** (Free order fee). The payment's own
  `feeAmount` stays `0`. Sandbox orders are never charged.
- You receive [`PAYMENT_FREE`](/guides/webhooks#free-orders) on the endpoints
  that selected it, and never `PAYMENT_PAID`. The conversion plugins listen for
  `payment.paid`, so they do not report a free order as a purchase: the order
  reaches plugins as the `payment.free` event.
- In production, the payer gets the
  [receipt e-mail](#payer-e-mails-and-accounts) and the purchase page, as for
  a paid payment. The hosted checkout shows the order as confirmed and
  sends the payer to `completionUrl` with `yvx_status=FREE` and
  `yvx_amount_cents=0` (see [Signed success redirect](#signed-success-redirect)).
- `GET /v1/documents/payments/{paymentId}/receipt` returns
  `400 RECEIPT_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
- `GET /v1/payments?status=FREE` lists free orders.
- A free order never offers the [post-purchase upsell](#post-purchase-upsell),
  and a create with an `upsell` and a `couponCode` that covers the whole amount
  fails with `400 UPSELL_AMOUNT_TOO_HIGH`.

## Hosted checkout

Set `mode: "hosted"` to receive a `checkoutUrl`. Redirect the payer there;
YuvexPay handles method selection, card capture and PIX rendering.

```json
{
  "amount": 49.90,
  "methods": ["PIX", "CARD"],
  "currency": "BRL",
  "mode": "hosted",
  "description": "Order #1234",
  "returnUrl": "https://store.example.com/checkout/return",
  "completionUrl": "https://store.example.com/checkout/done",
  "metadata": {
    "cancelUrl": "https://store.example.com/cart"
  },
  "customer": {
    "name": "Pedro Álvares Cabral",
    "email": "user@example.com",
    "document": "12345678900"
  }
}
```

- `returnUrl`, an `http` or `https` URL of up to 2048 characters, is the back
  link the hosted checkout shows while the payment is unpaid, so the payer can
  leave without paying. `metadata.cancelUrl` takes precedence when both are set.
- `completionUrl`, an `http` or `https` URL of up to 2048 characters, is where
  the checkout sends the payer after a successful payment, with signed query
  parameters (see [Signed success redirect](#signed-success-redirect)).
- `metadata.cancelUrl`, an `http` or `https` URL of up to 2048 characters,
  shows a back link to that URL on the hosted checkout while the payment is
  unpaid, including after it expires or is cancelled. Any other value is
  ignored, not rejected. The cancel URL of a payment link created in the
  dashboard works the same way on the payments the link creates.

Hosted payments start in `PENDING_METHOD_SELECTION` until the payer picks a
method, then transition through the same lifecycle as a headless payment. If
the payer doesn't pick a method before `expiresAt`, the payment moves to
`EXPIRED` shortly after and you receive `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`.

While the payment waits for a method, the checkout shows the order: the
[line items](#line-items), with buttons to change each quantity when
`itemsQuantityEditable` is true, the **Cupom** field when a
[coupon](#coupons) can apply, the [order bump](#order-bump), the discount and
the total of each method. Each change is saved on the payment, which computes
`amount`, the fee and the percentage splits again. Once the payer picks a
method, the order no longer changes. After the payment, the checkout can offer
a [post-purchase upsell](#post-purchase-upsell).

### Store name and branding

The payer sees your store name in the checkout header and legal line, on the
purchase page, in receipts and in the e-mails YuvexPay sends them, which come
from **&lt;store name&gt; via YuvexPay**. The store name is the company's
display name, **Nome da loja** (Store name), from 2 to 60 characters, asked
when the company is created. Change it, the logo and the color under
**Configurações &gt; Checkout** (Settings &gt; Checkout), on the
**Marca do checkout** (Checkout branding) card.

- A company registered with a CPF never shows the holder's name or CPF. A
  store name that contains either is refused.
- A company with a CNPJ also shows its razão social and CNPJ in the legal line.
- A company without a valid store name shows **YuvexPay** until one is set.

### Card payments

The hosted checkout is the only way to accept a card. `POST /v1/payments`
rejects card data, and `CARD` in headless mode, with
`400 CARD_PAYMENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE_VIA_API` (see
[Payment errors](/guides/errors#payment-errors)), so your servers never handle
or store card data.

To charge a card:

1. Create the payment with `mode: "hosted"` and `CARD` in `methods`: `["CARD"]`,
   or `["PIX", "CARD"]` to let the payer choose. The `amount` must be at least
   `R$ 5.00`.
2. Redirect the payer to the returned `checkoutUrl`. The checkout collects the
   card and runs 3D Secure itself. It accepts credit cards, charged in a
   single installment, and debit cards.
3. Read the result from the `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED`, `PAYMENT_PAID`,
   `PAYMENT_EXPIRED` and `PAYMENT_CANCELLED` webhooks, or poll
   `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}`.

`returnUrl` and `completionUrl` work as for any hosted payment. A hosted card
payment whose 3D Secure challenge fails, or gets no answer for about 7
minutes, ends `EXPIRED` and you receive `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`. A card refused after
the challenge ends `CANCELLED` and you receive `PAYMENT_CANCELLED` with
`reason: "payment_failed"`. In both cases the payer cannot pick another method
for that payment: create a new one.

Payment links created in the dashboard open the same hosted checkout.

Card payments must be enabled for your account; until they are, a request with
`CARD` in `methods` returns `403 PAYMENT_METHOD_NOT_ENABLED`. Card acceptance
also requires the account owner's e-mail to be confirmed, and YuvexPay's risk
controls can suspend it temporarily. On an account with card payments enabled,
while card acceptance is unavailable, other methods keep working, and:

- The hosted checkout does not offer `CARD`, even on payments and payment links
  created while it was available.
- Creating a payment with `CARD` and other methods succeeds without `CARD`.
- Creating a payment whose only method is `CARD` returns
  `403 PAYMENT_METHOD_NOT_ENABLED` (see
  [Payment errors](/guides/errors#payment-errors)).

Card payments already created with card data through `POST /v1/payments` keep
working: `GET` returns their masked card `methodData`, and webhooks, refunds and
chargebacks are unchanged.

On a card payment, `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` returns
`methodData.cardFundingType`: `credit` when the card was charged as a credit
card, `debit` when it was charged as a debit card, and `null` when the funding
type is not known, including older payments. Webhook bodies do not carry it.

### Signed success redirect

When a hosted payment with a `completionUrl` reaches `PAID`, `CONFIRMED` or
`FREE`, the checkout shows the payment as approved, counts down 5 seconds and
sends the payer to `completionUrl`. An **Ir agora** (Go now) button skips the
countdown. While a [post-purchase upsell](#post-purchase-upsell) is offered,
the checkout shows only the **Ir agora** button, and the countdown starts once
the payer declines the offer. On a payment link created in the dashboard, the
link's success URL takes the place of `completionUrl`. Only an `http` or
`https` URL of up to 2048 characters is used.

The checkout adds these query parameters to the URL:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| `yvx_payment_id` | The payment `id`. |
| `yvx_status` | `PAID`, `CONFIRMED` or `FREE`. |
| `yvx_amount_cents` | The payment `amount` in centavos, as an integer: `4990` for `49.90`, and `0` on a `FREE` payment. It is the final amount, after the coupon and any change the payer made on the checkout, and it does not include the fee added for the payer when `feePassedToPayer` is true. |
| `yvx_reference` | `metadata.externalReference` when the payment has it, which is where a payment link puts its reference; otherwise the `externalId` you sent; otherwise empty. The parameter is sent even when it is empty. |
| `yvx_ts` | Unix time, in seconds, when the URL was signed. |
| `yvx_signature` | Lowercase hex HMAC-SHA256 of the five parameters above. |

The query parameters already in your `completionUrl` are kept, and any `yvx_`
parameter in it is replaced. The URL is signed again each time the checkout
loads, so the same payment can reach your URL more than once, after a reload
or when the payer opens the checkout again, each time with a new `yvx_ts` and
`yvx_signature`. Sandbox payments redirect the same way, with the same secret.

#### Redirect secret

The signing key is your account's redirect secret, a 64-character string shown
in the dashboard under **Configurações &gt; Checkout** (Settings &gt; Checkout),
in **Segredo do redirecionamento** (Redirect secret), to users who can edit
webhooks. It is not the secret of a webhook endpoint. It is created the first
time that page is opened or a payment redirects, whichever comes first.

**Trocar segredo** (Rotate secret) replaces it at once, with no overlap: a URL
signed before the rotation, for example on a checkout page that was already
open, no longer verifies. Keep the secret on your server and never send it to
the browser.

#### Verifying the redirect

```
message   = yvx_payment_id + "." + yvx_status + "." + yvx_amount_cents + "." + yvx_reference + "." + yvx_ts
signature = hex(HMAC_SHA256(secret, message))
```

The key is the secret string exactly as shown, as UTF-8 bytes: do not
hex-decode it. Build the message from the URL-decoded parameter values.
`yvx_reference` can contain dots, so never split the message to read the
values back.

1. Read the six parameters. Reject the request if one is missing.
   `yvx_reference` can be empty, but it is always present.
2. Reject it if `yvx_ts` is more than **600 seconds** away from your clock.
3. Compute the signature and compare it with `yvx_signature` in constant time.
4. Look up your order by `yvx_payment_id` and compare `yvx_amount_cents` with
   the order total in cents. When the payer can change the order on the
   checkout, with a coupon, editable quantities or an order bump, compare it
   with the `amount` of `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` instead, and read what
   was bought from `items`.
5. Confirm the payment on your server before you fulfil the order: call
   [`GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}`](/api-reference/payments/get), or wait for
   the `PAYMENT_PAID`, `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` or `PAYMENT_FREE`
   [webhook](/guides/webhooks).
   Fulfil each payment once, since the redirect can repeat and the webhook
   arrives as well.

<Warning>
  The redirect brings the payer back to your site. It is not proof of payment:
  a payer who closes the tab during the countdown never reaches your URL, and a
  signed URL can be opened again until it is 600 seconds old. Fulfil orders
  from the API or the webhook.
</Warning>

<CodeGroup>
```javascript Node.js
import crypto from "crypto";

const MAX_AGE_SECONDS = 600;

function signSuccessRedirect(secret, { paymentId, status, amountCents, reference, ts }) {
  const message = `${paymentId}.${status}.${amountCents}.${reference}.${ts}`;
  return crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret).update(message, "utf8").digest("hex");
}

function verifySuccessRedirect(params, secret) {
  const paymentId = params.get("yvx_payment_id");
  const status = params.get("yvx_status");
  const amountCents = params.get("yvx_amount_cents");
  const reference = params.get("yvx_reference");
  const ts = params.get("yvx_ts");
  const signature = params.get("yvx_signature");
  const values = [paymentId, status, amountCents, reference, ts, signature];
  if (values.some((value) => typeof value !== "string")) return false;

  const now = Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000);
  if (!/^\d+$/.test(ts) || Math.abs(now - Number(ts)) > MAX_AGE_SECONDS) return false;

  const expected = signSuccessRedirect(secret, { paymentId, status, amountCents, reference, ts });
  const a = Buffer.from(signature, "utf8");
  const b = Buffer.from(expected, "utf8");
  return a.length === b.length && crypto.timingSafeEqual(a, b);
}

app.get("/checkout/done", async (req, res) => {
  const params = new URL(req.originalUrl, "https://store.example.com").searchParams;
```

### Skipping checkout steps

The hosted checkout asks the payer for their name, e-mail and CPF or CNPJ
before it shows the payment methods. When you already sent them on the
payment, add `skip_customer=1` to the `checkoutUrl` and the checkout goes
straight to the payment methods:

```
https://checkout.yuvexpay.com/checkout/fa1c0a85-7c29-4e3a-9e07-5b1a7b6d1234?skip_customer=1
```

- The form is skipped only when the payment has a customer, from `customer` or
  `customerId` on `POST /v1/payments`, with a name of 2 to 120 characters, a
  valid e-mail and a CPF or CNPJ with valid check digits. Otherwise the checkout
  shows the form as usual.
- `1` and `true` turn a flag on. Any other value is ignored.
- Once the checkout has saved details the payer filled in on a payment, the
  flag no longer skips the form on that payment.
- A card payment still asks for the name on the card and a phone number.
- When the payment has such a customer, the wait for
  [`CHECKOUT_ABANDONED`](/guides/webhooks#abandoned-checkouts) and the
  [recovery kit](#recovery-kit) starts the first time the checkout is opened
  while the payment is waiting for a method, with or without `skip_customer=1`.
- `skip_address=1` is accepted but has no effect today. `POST /v1/payments` has
  no address fields, so there is never an address to skip, and a card payment
  keeps asking for the CEP and the street number.
- Personal data in the URL is ignored. The checkout never fills the form from
  query parameters such as `name`, `email` or `document`: send the payer's data
  in `customer` when you create the payment.

The flags are read on the `checkoutUrl` only, not on the URL of a payment link.

### Recovery kit

The recovery kit e-mails a payer who filled in the hosted checkout and did not
pay, with a link back to the payment. Turn it on for a payment with
`recoveryKit` on `POST /v1/payments`:

```json
{
  "amount": 49.90,
  "methods": ["PIX", "CARD"],
  "mode": "hosted",
  "expiresInMinutes": 2880,
  "recoveryKit": true
}
```

- `true` turns the kit on for the payment and `false` turns it off. Without
  `recoveryKit`, the payment follows the account default: **Ativar por padrão**
  (Turn on by default) on the **Kit de recuperação** (Recovery kit) card under
  **Configurações &gt; Checkout**, which is off until you turn it on. A payment
  link created in the dashboard has its own **Kit de recuperação** setting in
  the link form: **Padrão da conta** (Account default), **Ativado** (On) or
  **Desativado** (Off).
- The kit starts when the hosted checkout saves the payer's name, e-mail and
  CPF or CNPJ, or the first time the checkout is opened for a payment that
  already has them (see [Skipping checkout steps](#skipping-checkout-steps)).
  This is the same moment that starts the wait for
  [`CHECKOUT_ABANDONED`](/guides/webhooks#abandoned-checkouts). A headless
  payment never starts a kit, so `recoveryKit: true` does nothing on it.
- The e-mails go to the e-mail the payer typed on the checkout, else the one
  you sent in `customer`. Up to 5 are sent, at the delays set under **Envios**
  (E-mails) on the same card, counted from the moment the kit started: 15
  minutes, 1 hour and 24 hours unless you change them. Each delay is between 5
  minutes and 7 days.
- When the payer picked PIX, one more e-mail goes out about 10 minutes before
  the PIX code expires.
- The e-mails stop when the payment is paid, and when it expires or is
  cancelled: an e-mail is sent only while the payment can still be paid, so
  set `expiresInMinutes` to cover the delays you want.
- On a payment from a payment link, the e-mails go on after the payment
  expires, with a link to the payment link, for as long as the link is active.
  They stop when the same payer, identified by e-mail, pays another payment of
  that link. A payer who opens the same link again while an earlier kit for
  their e-mail is still running gets no second kit, and the new payment shows
  `recoveryKit.enabled: false`.
- An e-mail is skipped when the payer unsubscribed from your store's e-mails
  or reached YuvexPay's sending limits for their address. YuvexPay also stops
  sending payer e-mails to an address, for every store, after a hard bounce, a
  block for an invalid address or a spam report. See
  [Payer e-mails and accounts](#payer-e-mails-and-accounts).
- Sandbox payments never get a kit: `recoveryKit.enabled` is `false` on them,
  even when you send `true`.

The kit has a fixed price per payment, shown in the dashboard on the
**Kit de recuperação** card. It is charged once, when the first e-mail of the
kit is sent, whether or not the payer pays afterwards, and it is debited from
your balance even if the balance goes negative. The price charged is the one in
effect when the kit started, and a kit that sends no e-mail is not charged. The
debit appears in [`GET /v1/documents/statements`](/api-reference/documents/statement)
as **Kit de recuperação**.

`GET /v1/payments`, `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` and
`GET /v1/payments/txid/{txId}` return the kit of each payment:

```json
"recoveryKit": {
  "enabled": true,
  "charged": true,
  "emailsSent": 2
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `enabled` | The kit is on for this payment. `false` when it was created with the kit off, in sandbox, or before the kit existed. |
| `charged` | The kit price was debited from your balance. |
| `emailsSent` | Recovery e-mails sent for this payment, the one before a PIX code expires included. |

The dashboard shows the results per day on the **Recuperação de vendas**
(Sales recovery) card of **Visão geral** (Overview). The detail of a payment
shows its kit, with a **Ligar kit** (Turn on kit) or **Parar e-mails** (Stop
e-mails) button.

### Order bump

An order bump is one more product offered on the hosted checkout, as a box the
payer ticks before picking a method. Send `orderBump` on a hosted payment:

```json
{
  "items": [{ "name": "Camiseta", "unitAmount": 59.90, "quantity": 1 }],
  "methods": ["PIX", "CARD"],
  "mode": "hosted",
  "orderBump": {
    "name": "Meias",
    "unitAmount": 19.90,
    "description": "Par de meias da mesma coleção",
    "productId": "8a3f1c2e-6b4d-4e7a-9c1b-2d3e4f5a6b7c"
  }
}
```

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| `name` | 1 to 120 characters. |
| `unitAmount` | Price in BRL, greater than `0`, with at most 2 decimal places. |
| `description` | Optional, up to 500 characters. |
| `productId` | Optional. A product of your account in the environment of the key, whose image is shown with the offer. Any other id returns `400 OFFER_PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`. |

- When the payer ticks the box, the bump joins `items` with
  `kind: "ORDER_BUMP"` and quantity `1`, and `amount`, the fee and the
  percentage splits are computed again. Unticked, it leaves the order. The
  payer can change it until they pick a method.
- A coupon for the whole order covers the bump. A coupon limited to products
  covers it only when its `productId` is in the coupon's `productIds`.
- A payment created with an amount only can have a bump too: the bump is added
  to that amount.
- `orderBump` and `upsell` are for hosted payments only. In headless mode, the
  default, they return `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` with `OFFERS_REQUIRE_HOSTED` in
  the message.
- The bump is not shown on a payment with a `FIXED` split.

The dashboard shows how often bumps and upsells are accepted, and the revenue
they add, on the **Order bump e upsell** card of **Visão geral** (Overview).
The order bump and upsell filled in when you create a payment link are set
under **Configurações &gt; Checkout**, on the **Ofertas para novos links**
(Offers for new links) card.

### Post-purchase upsell

An upsell is a second product offered on the hosted checkout right after the
payment is paid. Send `upsell` on a hosted payment, with the same fields as
`orderBump`:

```json
"upsell": {
  "name": "Moletom",
  "unitAmount": 99.90,
  "description": "Moletom da mesma coleção"
}
```

- `unitAmount` goes from `R$ 1.00` up to the payment `amount` after the
  coupon. Above it, the request fails with `400 UPSELL_AMOUNT_TOO_HIGH`.
- The offer appears on the success screen of a `PAID` or `CONFIRMED` payment
  for 30 minutes, counted from the payment for PIX and from the card charge for
  a card. The payer sees it once: after they accept or decline, it is gone.
- When the payer accepts, YuvexPay creates a new payment and takes the payer to
  its checkout. If the original was paid by card in the same browser, the payer
  can pay in one click with the same card, which goes through the same checks
  as any card payment. Otherwise, the payer pays by PIX. PIX is offered when the
  original payment accepted it, and the card only for an upsell of at least
  `R$ 5.00`.
- The new payment has its own `id` and `txId`, `parentPaymentId` set to the
  original payment, one item with `kind: "UPSELL"`, the offer name as
  `description`, the same payer and the same splits. It expires in 30 minutes,
  never passes the fee to the payer, has no recovery kit and sends its own
  webhooks.
- The upsell is not offered on a payment with a `FIXED` split, or with a split
  whose partner relationship is no longer active, nor on a
  [free order](#free-orders). It is not offered either when the payer's changes
  on the checkout, such as a lower quantity or a coupon, took the final
  `amount` below the upsell price.

<Note>
  The upsell payment is created by YuvexPay, not by your integration. It keeps
  `metadata.cancelUrl` but none of your other `metadata` keys, and it has no
  `externalId`, so its webhooks arrive for an `id` you never created. Read it
  with `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` and link it to your order through
  `parentPaymentId`. Once it is paid, the checkout sends the payer to the same
  `completionUrl`, with the upsell payment's `yvx_payment_id` and an empty
  `yvx_reference`.
</Note>

## Attribution tracking

Send the optional `tracking` object on `POST /v1/payments` to attribute the
sale to the campaign and the visitor that brought the payer. YuvexPay stores it
with the payment and passes it to the conversion plugins enabled for your
account, such as GA4, Meta Conversions API, TikTok Events API and UTMify.

```json
{
  "amount": 49.90,
  "methods": ["PIX"],
  "mode": "headless",
  "tracking": {
    "utmSource": "facebook",
    "utmMedium": "cpc",
    "utmCampaign": "black-friday",
    "fbclid": "IwAR2F4-dbP0l7Mn1IawQQGCINEz7PYXQvwjNwB_qa2ofrHyiLjcbCRxTDMgk",
    "gaClientId": "1234567890.1761900000",
    "fbp": "fb.1.1761900000000.1116446470",
    "fbc": "fb.1.1761900000000.IwAR2F4-dbP0l7Mn1IawQQGCINEz7PYXQvwjNwB_qa2ofrHyiLjcbCRxTDMgk"
  }
}
```

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| `utmSource`, `utmMedium`, `utmCampaign`, `utmTerm`, `utmContent` | The `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term` and `utm_content` parameters of the landing URL. Up to 512 characters each. |
| `src`, `sck` | The `src` and `sck` parameters of the landing URL. Up to 512 characters each. |
| `fbclid` | Meta click id. Up to 512 characters. |
| `gclid` | Google Ads click id. Up to 512 characters. |
| `ttclid` | TikTok click id. Up to 1000 characters. |
| `gaClientId` | GA4 client id, `<digits>.<digits>`. A raw `_ga` cookie value, `GA1.1.<a>.<b>`, is accepted too and stored as `<a>.<b>`. |
| `fbp` | Value of the Meta `_fbp` cookie, `fb.<subdomainIndex>.<creationTime>.<random>`. |
| `fbc` | Value of the Meta `_fbc` cookie, `fb.<subdomainIndex>.<creationTime>.<fbclid>`. |

Every field is optional, and values are trimmed. A `gaClientId`, `fbp` or `fbc`
that does not match its format is ignored, and the payment is created without
it. Any other field that is not a string or is over its length limit fails the
request with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`. When you send `fbclid` without `fbc`,
YuvexPay builds the `fbc` for Meta from it.

### Tracking on the hosted checkout

The hosted checkout fills `tracking` by itself from the URL the payer opens it
with: the `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term`,
`utm_content`, `src`, `sck`, `fbclid`, `gclid` and `ttclid` query parameters,
and `_ga`, `_fbp` and `_fbc` as query parameters or as cookies of the
checkout's own domain. The fields you sent in `tracking` when you created the
payment are kept: the checkout fills only the ones you left empty. Payment
links created in the dashboard capture the same parameters from the link URL.

Your own `_ga`, `_fbp` and `_fbc` cookies are set on your domain, so the
checkout cannot read them. To attribute a hosted-checkout purchase to the
visitor your GA4 tag and Meta Pixel saw, send those cookie values in `tracking`
when you create the payment, or append them, URL-encoded, to the `checkoutUrl`
as query parameters:

```
https://checkout.yuvexpay.com/checkout/fa1c0a85-7c29-4e3a-9e07-5b1a7b6d1234?_ga=GA1.1.1234567890.1761900000&_fbp=fb.1.1761900000000.1116446470
```

## Fees and net amount

Each payment response includes:

- `amount` — the gross amount charged to the payer.
- `feeAmount` — the YuvexPay processing fee.
- `netAmount` — the amount credited to your balance once the payment settles,
  before any [splits](#splits).

Fees are computed at creation time using the merchant's configured rate.
`passFeeToPayer: true` shifts the fee onto the payer (the payer is charged
`amount + feeAmount`); when `false`, the fee is deducted from `amount`.

On a hosted payment, the fee in the create response is the one of the first
method in `methods`. The checkout shows each method with its own total, and
when the payer picks a method, `feeAmount`, `payerFeeAmount` and `netAmount`
are computed again at that method's rate, card included. Cards are charged in a
single installment, so no installment fee is added. The fee is also computed
again whenever the order changes on the checkout. Below `R$ 1.00`, the fee is
always passed to the payer. A [free order](#free-orders) has a fee of its own,
never passed to the payer.

A refund you issue returns the fee in proportion to the refunded amount, unless
it takes the counted volume of your verified CPF or CNPJ below a rewards prize
YuvexPay approved for it (see [refunds below a redeemed rewards prize](/guides/refunds#refunds-below-a-redeemed-rewards-prize)).
A PIX MED that returns funds, a chargeback and a refund YuvexPay makes after a
[refund request from the payer](/guides/refunds#refunds-requested-by-the-payer)
do not: your balance is debited the full amount returned to the payer, and
YuvexPay keeps `feeAmount`.

## Splits

A payment can hand part of itself to other YuvexPay companies. Send `splits` on
`POST /v1/payments`; each entry names the company that receives a share and how
much of the payment it gets.

```json
{
  "amount": 49.90,
  "methods": ["PIX"],
  "mode": "headless",
  "splits": [
    {
      "recipientCompanyId": "b7f1c3d2-4e5a-4b6c-8d9e-0a1b2c3d4e5f",
      "type": "PERCENTAGE",
      "value": 10
    },
    {
      "recipientCompanyId": "c9a2d4e6-8b1f-4c3a-9d7e-2f5a8b0c1d3e",
      "type": "FIXED",
      "value": 5.00
    }
  ]
}
```

| Field | Value | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `recipientCompanyId` | uuid | The YuvexPay company that receives the share. Must be a company other than your own, and unique within the array. |
| `type` | `PERCENTAGE` or `FIXED` | How `value` is read. Both forms can coexist in the same array. |
| `value` | `0.01`–`100` for `PERCENTAGE`; `0.01` or more for `FIXED` | A percentage of `amount`, or a BRL amount. At most two decimal places either way. |

`splits` is optional. The maximum number of entries is the recipient limit
configured for your account — three by default, and support can raise it.
Splits are set at creation only: there is no endpoint to add, edit or remove one
afterwards. The create response and the payment detail response echo them back
as `splits`, each entry carrying an `id` alongside the three fields you sent,
and `[]` when the payment has none.

Percentage shares follow the final `amount`, after a [coupon](#coupons) and the
payer's changes on the hosted checkout. A payment with a `FIXED` split takes no
coupon, keeps its quantities, and offers no order bump and no upsell. A
[post-purchase upsell](#post-purchase-upsell) payment carries the same splits
as the original.

### How the money divides

The YuvexPay fee is always the originating company's and is never apportioned.
Shares are computed from `amount` — the gross — and the fee comes out of your
share alone. When `passFeeToPayer` is true, nothing is deducted from `amount` at
all.

`netAmount` stays pre-split: it is still `amount - feeAmount`, or `amount` when
the fee is passed to the payer. You keep `netAmount` minus the sum of the
splits, and the recipients are credited when the payment settles.

For `amount: 49.90` against a `2.99` fee, split 10% to one company and a fixed
`R$ 5.00` to another:

| Field | Value | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `amount` | `49.90` | The gross. Percentage shares are computed from it. |
| `feeAmount` | `2.99` | Yours. Never apportioned. |
| `netAmount` | `46.91` | `amount - feeAmount`, before splits. |
| First split (`PERCENTAGE`, `10`) | `4.99` | 10% of `49.90`. |
| Second split (`FIXED`, `5.00`) | `5.00` | Taken as sent. |
| Your share | `36.92` | `netAmount` minus both splits. |

The sum of the splits must be at most `netAmount`. A request that goes over
fails with `SPLIT_AMOUNT_EXCEEDS_NET` and the response names the overflow.
Nothing is clamped.

Percentage shares are rounded **down** to the cent, and every remaining cent
stays with the originating company. On `amount: 100.00` with a `2.99` fee and
one recipient at `33.33%`, the recipient is credited `33.33` and you keep
`97.01 - 33.33 = 63.68`.

### Split partners

You can only split to a company that has agreed to receive splits from you. The
pair is created in the dashboard, under **Configurações &gt; Parceiros de
divisão**: the originating company invites the recipient by its **company ID**
— the same ID the recipient sees under **Configurações &gt; Empresa** — and the
recipient accepts. The recipient never initiates.

Consent is per environment and per direction. A recipient that accepted in
production still has to accept in sandbox, and accepting your splits does not
let it split to you.

[`GET /v1/splits/partners`](/api-reference/splits/partners) lists the companies
your account may split with, so an integration knows which IDs are usable
without hardcoding them. Inviting, accepting, declining and revoking are
dashboard-only: accepting is a human act.

There is one exception. A platform approved by YuvexPay connects a seller on
the seller's first payment, with no invitation, by sending its own API key in
the `X-Split-Platform-Key` header of `POST /v1/payments`. The connection works
in production and in sandbox, with a key of the same environment as the
seller's, and carries a fee cap: a split to an approved platform above the cap
fails with `SPLIT_FEE_CAP_EXCEEDED`, and one to a platform whose approval was
withdrawn fails with `SPLIT_PLATFORM_SUSPENDED`. See
[Split platforms](/guides/split-platforms).

A relationship is in one of five states.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | Invited, waiting for the recipient to answer. |
| `ACCEPTED` | Active. Only this state can be named in `splits[]`. |
| `DECLINED` | The recipient said no. |
| `REVOKED` | An active pair was ended, by either side. |
| `CANCELLED` | The originator withdrew the invite before it was answered. |

Invites do not expire; the originator can cancel one at any time. After
`DECLINED`, `REVOKED` or `CANCELLED` the originator can invite the same company
again — that starts a **new** relationship and the earlier one is kept as
history.

Creating a payment naming a recipient that has not accepted fails with
`SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_PENDING`, one naming a recipient that declined the
invitation fails with `SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_DECLINED`, and one naming a recipient
whose pair was revoked fails with `SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_REVOKED`. Revoked is
reported ahead of declined, and declined ahead of pending.
`SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_DECLINED` carries `details.recipients`, the company IDs that
refused; the pair becomes usable again only once you send a new invitation from
the dashboard and that company accepts it.

`SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_PENDING` is still the single answer for every other
recipient that cannot receive a split yet. Declined and revoked are only
reachable for a company your own company invited first, so neither tells you
anything you did not already know, and a company you never invited reads the
same as a company ID that belongs to no company at all — so the error cannot be
used to discover which company IDs exist.

<Note>
  Revocation is forward-only, and either side can revoke. Payments that were
  already created still settle, refund and freeze normally, and the recipient
  still receives those shares — a split is fixed at creation.
</Note>

Your company can hold a limited number of split requests awaiting an answer, 20
by default and counted per environment. Beyond that, naming a new recipient on
a payment fails with `SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_REQUEST_LIMIT` until some of the
outstanding requests are answered.

The number of *active* partners is capped too: three by default, the same
recipient limit that bounds `splits[]`. The dashboard enforces that ceiling
when an invite is sent or accepted. The API returns it in one case: a
[platform connection](/guides/split-platforms) that would take the seller past
the limit fails with `SPLIT_PARTNER_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, and `details.limit`
carries the limit.

Both companies must be approved before a split payment can be created: KYC for
an individual, KYC and KYB for a business, on both sides. The bar is checked
when the invite is sent and again when the payment is created, not when it
settles. Your own company failing it returns
`403 SPLIT_ORIGINATOR_NOT_APPROVED`. A recipient that has not accepted returns
`SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_PENDING` whatever the reason, or
`SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_DECLINED` if it refused your invitation; one that had
accepted and has since stopped meeting the bar returns
`SPLIT_RECIPIENT_NOT_APPROVED`.

In sandbox the flow is identical but the KYC gate does not apply, so the whole
invite → accept → split cycle is testable with unverified sandbox companies.

### What a recipient sees

A split is not the recipient's payment, and recipients get no webhook and no
receipt for it.

- `GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}` returns `404` for a recipient: the payment
  belongs to the originating company.
- The payment receipt is originator-only.
- Recipients see the money in [`GET /v1/balance`](/api-reference/balance/get)
  and in [`GET /v1/documents/statements`](/api-reference/documents/statement),
  and in their dashboard.

<Warning>
  Don't build a recipient integration that waits for `PAYMENT_PAID`. No webhook
  is delivered to a recipient for a split. Read the balance or the statement
  instead.
</Warning>

### Refunds, MED and reserve

Refunds and PIX MED are proportional. A refund debits every party in proportion
to what it received, the originating company included, and partial refunds use
the same proportions. A MED freezes each party's proportional share.

A recipient that has already withdrawn its share goes negative. The shortfall is
never moved onto the originating company, and
`INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND` is measured against the originating company's
share only.

A split credit is subject to the **recipient's own** rolling reserve policy, not
the originator's. When a refund or MED debit lands on a recipient whose share is
still in reserve, that payment's own reserve hold is released first and the
debit is written against it.

### Sandbox

A sandbox payment can only split to a company that is in sandbox mode, and a
production payment only to a production company. The other direction is always
rejected, never a silent no-op: `400 SPLIT_RELATIONSHIP_PENDING` for a recipient
that has not accepted in this environment, and `400 SPLIT_ENVIRONMENT_MISMATCH`
for one that had. Sandbox splits never touch a production balance, statement or
report.

The invite, accept and split cycle works in sandbox, and so does the
[platform connection](/guides/split-platforms#sandbox): the platform sends a
`ypk_test_` key of its approved company in `X-Split-Platform-Key`, the same fee
cap applies and the seller is not notified. Once connected in sandbox, the
approved platform, a production company, can be named in the seller's sandbox
payments: it is the one exception to the sandbox-mode rule above.

## Pagination and filtering

`GET /v1/payments` supports the standard `page` (default `1`) and `limit`
(default `20`, max `100`) parameters along with these filters:

- `status` — filter by `PaymentStatus`, `FREE` included.
- `method` — filter by `PIX`, `CARD` or `BOLETO`.
- `startDate` / `endDate` — ISO 8601 date or date-time bounds.
- `minAmount` / `maxAmount` — decimal BRL bounds.
- `environment` — `SANDBOX` or `PRODUCTION` (defaults to the API key's environment).

## Payer e-mails and accounts

- **Receipt e-mail.** In production, YuvexPay e-mails the payer a receipt after
  they pay. **E-mail de recibo ao comprador** (Buyer receipt e-mail), under
  **Configurações &gt; Checkout**, turns it off. It is on by default.
- **Addresses that stop receiving e-mails.** YuvexPay stops sending payer
  e-mails to an address after a hard bounce, a block for an invalid address or
  a spam report, for every store. An address that unsubscribed from your
  store's e-mails gets none of them, receipts and
  [recovery kit](#recovery-kit) e-mails included.
- **Payer accounts.** A payer can sign in to YuvexPay with their e-mail,
  download their data as JSON and delete their account. Deletion requires the
  payer to confirm their identity again, and is refused while a payment linked
  to the account has a pending charge, a refund in progress, an open dispute,
  MED or chargeback, or an open refund request. The payment records kept for
  financial and anti-money-laundering retention stay. The rest of the
  account's data is erased or anonymized, and a running recovery kit on those
  payments stops.

## Receipts

Once a payment is `PAID` or `REFUNDED`, you can fetch a printable receipt:

```
GET /v1/documents/payments/{paymentId}/receipt?format=html
```

See the [Documents reference](/api-reference/documents/payment-receipt) for the
full response shape. A [free order](#free-orders) has no receipt here: the
request returns `400 RECEIPT_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
