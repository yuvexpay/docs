---
title: "Refunds"
description: "Reverse a payment back to the payer, fully or partially."
---

A refund returns funds to the payer's source of funds. The money comes out of
your YuvexPay balance — or, when the payment carries
[splits](/guides/payments#splits), out of every party's balance in proportion to
what it received. Refunds are issued via
`POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund`.

## What can be refunded

The eligibility rules are enforced server-side when you call
`POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund`. The payment detail response does not
carry an eligibility field: an ineligible payment is reported by the refund
call itself.

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Payment is `PAID` | Refundable. |
| Payment is `PARTIAL_REFUND` | Not refundable. A payment can be refunded once (`REFUND_ALREADY_PERFORMED`). |
| Payment is `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`, `CHARGEBACK`, or `MED_FROZEN` | Not refundable. |
| Payment needs a manual review before it can be refunded | `REFUND_REQUIRES_MANUAL_REVIEW`. Contact support. |
| Payment was processed through a route that does not support refunds | `REFUND_NOT_SUPPORTED`. |
| Insufficient available balance to cover the refund | `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND`. On a split payment only the originating company's share is checked. |

A refund issued for a non-eligible payment returns a `400` with one of the
codes above.

## Issue a refund

Send no `amount` to refund the full payment, or an `amount` between `0.01` and
the payment amount for a partial refund. A payment can be refunded once, fully
or partially: after a partial refund, the rest of the payment cannot be
refunded.

Partial refunds are conditional: they require a payment that has already settled
**and** a processing route that supports them. When either condition is missing
the request fails with `PARTIAL_REFUND_NOT_SUPPORTED` and the payment stays
fully refundable. A full refund is always available on a refundable payment.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.yuvexpay.com/v1/payments/5d0f8b6e-3a02-4f5b-9e1c-7c6a4a1b8c9d/refund \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $YUVEX_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-Idempotency-Key: refund-order-1234" \
  -d '{
    "reason": "Customer requested cancellation"
  }'
```

The response contains the refund record:

```json
{
  "refund": {
    "id": "fa1c0a85-7c29-4e3a-9e07-5b1a7b6d1234",
    "txId": "REF_3f2a8b9c4e6d1f5a7b3c8d2e9f4a6b1c",
    "amount": 49.90,
    "reason": "Customer requested cancellation",
    "status": "PROCESSING",
    "processedAt": null,
    "createdAt": "2026-05-03T14:21:08.443Z"
  }
}
```

## Refunds on split payments

When the payment carries [splits](/guides/payments#splits), the refund is
apportioned: every party is debited in proportion to what it received, the
originating company included. A partial refund uses the same proportions as a
full one.

A recipient that has already withdrawn its share goes negative. The shortfall is
never moved onto the originating company, and
`INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND` is evaluated against the originating
company's share only — a recipient never blocks a refund. When a debit lands on
a share that is still in the recipient's rolling reserve, that payment's reserve
hold is released first and the debit is written against it.

A PIX MED freezes each party's proportional share the same way.

## Fees on refunds, MED and chargebacks

A refund you issue debits your balance net of the processing fee: the fee is
returned in proportion to the refunded amount, except in the case described in
[refunds below a redeemed rewards prize](#refunds-below-a-redeemed-rewards-prize).
A PIX MED that returns funds, a chargeback and a
[refund YuvexPay makes](#refunds-requested-by-the-payer) after a refund request
from the payer debit the full amount returned to the payer, and the processing
fee is not returned. On a split payment the debit of a PIX MED or a chargeback
is apportioned across the parties the same way.

If the card dispute is won, the amount debited for the chargeback is credited back to your balance, the processing fee stays with YuvexPay, and the payment returns to the status it had before the chargeback: `PAID`, `CONFIRMED`, `REFUNDED` or `PARTIAL_REFUND`. On a split payment each party is credited back the
share it was debited. You are notified in the dashboard, and the payment's
`PAYMENT_PAID` or `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` webhook is sent again for its restored
status; a payment restored to `REFUNDED` or `PARTIAL_REFUND` sends no webhook.

A cardholder can still open a chargeback on a card payment you already
refunded. The chargeback is debited from your balance, even if it goes
negative, and YuvexPay contests it with the refund receipt. If the dispute is
won, the refunded part is credited back to your balance.

### Refunds below a redeemed rewards prize

A refund you issue keeps the processing fee when your verified CPF or CNPJ has
redeemed a rewards prize that YuvexPay approved, and the refund takes that
document's counted volume below the threshold of the highest such prize. The
counted volume is the sum, across every company with that document, of the
production payments that are `PAID`, `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIAL_REFUND` or
`MED_FROZEN`, net of what was refunded, once they are 30 days old for PIX and
boleto and 90 days old for card. In that case:

- Your balance is debited the full amount returned to the payer, and YuvexPay
  keeps the processing fee in proportion to the refunded amount.
- `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND` is still evaluated against the debit of a
  normal refund. The kept fee is debited on top of it and can take your
  available balance negative.
- On a split payment, the kept fee is debited from the originating company
  only. The recipients are debited as in any other refund.
- If the refund fails at the underlying network, the whole amount, fee
  included, is returned to your balance.

## Refunds requested by the payer

In production, the payer of a paid payment can ask for a refund on the purchase
page YuvexPay shows them after they pay, for up to 90 days after the payment.
You answer the request in the dashboard, under
**Vendas &gt; Reembolsos solicitados** (Requested refunds). There is no API to
read or answer requests.

- Approving a request issues a full refund exactly like
  `POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund`: the same eligibility rules, the same
  fees and the same `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND` check. With
  **Aprovar automaticamente pedidos em até 7 dias** (Approve requests within 7
  days automatically) turned on under **Configurações &gt; Empresa**, a request
  made within 7 days of the payment is approved without you. If its refund
  cannot be issued, for example for lack of balance, the request stays open.
- If you refuse the request, do not answer it, or its refund fails, YuvexPay
  support can take it over. As a last resort, YuvexPay refunds the payment
  itself. That refund debits your balance the full amount returned to the
  payer, and YuvexPay keeps the processing fee, as with a PIX MED or a
  chargeback. It is not held back by `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND`, so it
  can take your available balance negative. You are notified in the dashboard.
- A payment with an open PIX MED or a chargeback cannot be refunded through a
  request, and an open request is closed when either is opened. A refund you
  issue yourself closes the open request of that payment once it completes.

Both arrive like any other refund: `PAYMENT_REFUNDED` or
`PAYMENT_REFUND_FAILED` with the usual body, and an entry in
`GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refunds`. The webhook body has no reason; the
refund's `reason` tells them apart:

| `reason` | Refund |
|----------|--------|
| `Pedido de reembolso do comprador` | A payer's request that you approved, or that was approved automatically. |
| `Reembolso feito pela YuvexPay` | The refund YuvexPay made after a payer's request. |

Because these refunds do not go through your integration, your own
`POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund` can meet one that is in progress
(`REFUND_IN_PROGRESS`) or already completed (`ALREADY_REFUNDED`). Update your
order from the webhook, not only from your own refund calls.

## Refund lifecycle

A refund can be in one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `PROCESSING` | The refund was accepted and is being executed at the underlying network. |
| `COMPLETED`  | The refund cleared and the payment moved to `REFUNDED` (or `PARTIAL_REFUND` after a partial refund). |
| `FAILED`     | The refund could not be executed. The funds are returned to your balance and you can retry. |

Listen for `PAYMENT_REFUNDED` and `PAYMENT_REFUND_FAILED` webhook events to
react to status changes.

## Idempotency

Sending `POST /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refund` requires an
`X-Idempotency-Key` header. Reusing the same key with the same body returns
the original response. Reusing the key with a different body returns
`409 IDEMPOTENCY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`.

While a refund is being processed for a given payment, additional refund
requests fail with `400 REFUND_IN_PROGRESS` until the current refund settles.
If it completes, the payment cannot be refunded again. If it fails, you can
refund the payment again.

## Listing refunds for a payment

```
GET /v1/payments/{paymentId}/refunds
```

Returns every refund that has been attempted or completed against a payment,
in chronological order. Useful for reconciling refund flows or showing a
history to your operations team.

`reason` is the one you sent with the refund, one of the two reasons in
[Refunds requested by the payer](#refunds-requested-by-the-payer), or `null`
when none was sent and on refunds that reached YuvexPay from the payment
network, such as the return of a PIX MED.

## Common error codes

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `INVALID_STATUS` | The payment is not in a refundable status. |
| `PARTIAL_REFUND_NOT_SUPPORTED` | The payment has not settled yet, or its processing route cannot execute partial refunds. Either omit `amount` or send the full remaining refundable amount. |
| `ALREADY_REFUNDED` | A previous refund has already returned the full balance. |
| `REFUND_ALREADY_PERFORMED` | The payment was already partially refunded. A payment can be refunded once. |
| `REFUND_IN_PROGRESS` | A refund is currently in flight against this payment. Retry only if that refund fails. |
| `REFUND_NOT_SUPPORTED` | This payment was processed via a method or route that doesn't support refunds via API. |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE_FOR_REFUND` | Your available balance is too low to cover the refund. On a split payment, the check covers your own proportional share only. |
| `PSP_REFUND_FAILED` | The underlying network rejected the refund. The funds remain on your balance and you can retry. |
| `REFUND_REQUIRES_MANUAL_REVIEW` | The payment needs a manual review before it can be refunded. Contact support. |
